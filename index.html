Tips

Fill the scan window with the barcode or the part-number line. Distributor labels (Digi-Key, Mouser, Farnell) carry a Data Matrix or Code 128 barcode with the manufacturer part number; those read instantly. Printed text takes a second or two and works best on flat, well-lit labels. If the camera does not start from the home-screen icon, open the same link in Safari.
